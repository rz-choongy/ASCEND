ASCEND brand options

Three colour directions, each shown on the Log screen. Pick one, then answer the three questions at the bottom. The accent stays user-selectable in Settings; these set the default and everything around it.

1. Colour pick one

Each option starts from something you see at the gym, so the app looks like climbing, not like a generic fitness dashboard.
