What changed in the graphs

  • Every graph you have today is still here: grade pyramid, sends, flash rate, frequency, bodyweight, records, strength volume and per-exercise progress.
  • New graph: hardest send by week. Your app already has a grade trend chart. This puts it on the main Progress view.
  • Data is drawn in Chalk (or Marker in light mode), with grey for the second series. The accent colour only marks the latest point, so the graphs work with any of the 14 accents.
  • Grade rows use the grade tape as labels, and every bar has its count written next to it, so nothing depends on colour alone.
  • Frequency is now bars, not a line. A line between weeks implies values in between that don't exist.
  • The flash-rate ring and the tiny sends chart are gone. The numbers say the same thing more clearly.
  • Streaks move from a big card into Records, as plain numbers. Your product brief says no streak features, so this keeps the data without making it a goal.
