Bright-style vs Gear B

Same data, same features, same pills. Scroll each row sideways. The Bright-style phones are live HTML, so hover the charts for values.

What Bright OS does

  • Soft grey canvas with a colour wash at the top
  • White cards with 20px corners, no heavy shadows
  • Big light-weight numbers with small grey units
  • Each metric has its own colour and icon
  • Segmented pill controls (D / W / M / 3M / Y)
  • Rings, gradient bars and a stage "barcode" for sleep
  • Floating pill tab bar
  • Pure black dark mode with bright data colours

Pure Bright-style looks the most modern but reads as a generic health app, and its thin numbers are harder to read from a wall.
