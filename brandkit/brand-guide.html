Brand guide · v0.1 · draft2 Nov 2026
Ascend
Climbing and training log

Built for the gym floor: chalky hands, loud room, a few seconds between attempts. The look comes from the wall itself: setter's tape, concrete, rubber mats and marker pen.

01

What it stands for

Every design call goes back to these. If a choice doesn't serve one of them, cut it.

Speed firstEvery screen serves the next log. A climb takes two taps.
Phone is truthWorks fully offline. Cloud sync is opt-in, never required.
Numbers leadGrades and counts are the content. Chrome stays quiet.
Honest, not hypeShow what happened. Never celebrate it. No streaks, badges or confetti.
Speak the gym's languageEach gym's own grades and colours, mapped to V grades.
